SILOS Fragmented Citizen Feedback

MISALIGNMENT Misaligned CapEx Spending

Millions in capital expenditure (CapEx) are allocated to low-demand projects while critical citizen infrastructure hotspots remain neglected.

GAPS Unaddressed Infrastructure Failures

Stormwater pipelines, failing power grids, and transit culverts remain unmonitored until severe emergencies or monsoon disasters strike.

NO DATA Missing Demographic & Risk Integration

No correlation engine connecting citizen feedback with population density census data, vulnerability indices, and live IMD weather risk vectors.

Digital Public Good (DPG) & Open Standards

Built for open governance, interoperability, and privacy
10 / 12

📜 OpenAPI 3.0 Webhook Specification

Fully compliant standard webhook endpoint (POST /api/v1/dpg/messaging-webhook) for instant integration with national messaging platforms.

🔒 Privacy & Security by Design

Anonymized citizen telemetry and salted PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 password hashing. Zero persistent tracking of sensitive personal identities.

🌐 UN SDG Strategic Alignment

Directly contributes to UN SDG 9 (Industry & Infrastructure), SDG 11 (Sustainable Cities), and SDG 16 (Peace, Justice & Strong Institutions).

🔓 Open Data & Interoperability

Exports structured JSON-LD schemas and GIS records compatible with open government portals like data.gov.in and dados.gov.br.
