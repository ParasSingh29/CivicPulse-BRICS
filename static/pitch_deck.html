The Challenge

Governance & CapEx Friction Across BRICS

Why government infrastructure investment keeps failing citizens

SILOS Fragmented Citizen Feedback

Petitions live in disconnected departmental channels, voice messages, and paper files with no unified aggregation engine across BRICS nations.

MISALIGN Misaligned CapEx Spending

Crores in capital expenditure go to low-demand projects while critical infrastructure hotspots remain starved of investment and attention.

GAPS Unaddressed Infrastructure Failures

Stormwater pipelines, power grids, and transit culverts stay unmonitored until monsoon disasters or structural collapses force action.

NO DATA Zero Demographic Integration

No engine connecting citizen grievances with population density, vulnerability indices, and live IMD weather risk vectors for smart prioritization.

AI Approach

Multi-Agent Intelligence Architecture

Powered by Google Gemini 2.5 Pro & Flash running four specialized AI agents

📸 Gemini Vision Defect Agent

Analyzes citizen-uploaded infrastructure photos — classifies defect type, assigns severity score (1–10), hazard rating, priority code, and recommended action within seconds.

🎙️ Multilingual Speech & Translation

Converts voice notes in Hindi, Portuguese, or Zulu to text, translates to English, and synthesizes audio responses with Google TTS (gTTS) across 5 languages.

📊 CapEx Budget Alignment Engine

Fuses citizen petition density with census demographics, IMD weather risk vectors, and public investment plans to produce a quantified budget misalignment score per district.

🤖 Gemini Policy Directive Agent

Synthesizes executive-grade CapEx reallocation proposals and national infrastructure megaplans for ministers — backed by GIS spatial demand evidence and demographic indices.

Open Standards

Digital Public Good (DPG) Compliance

Built for open governance, interoperability, privacy & UN SDG alignment from day one

📜 OpenAPI 3.0 Webhook Specification

Fully compliant POST /api/v1/dpg/messaging-webhook — instant integration with any national messaging platform worldwide.

🔒 Privacy & Security by Design

Anonymized citizen telemetry + salted PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 password hashing. Zero persistent tracking of sensitive personal data.

🔓 Open Data & Interoperability

JSON-LD schemas & GIS records export-compatible with data.gov.in, dados.gov.br, and all national open-government portals.

✅ DPGA Standard Aligned

Adheres to Digital Public Goods Alliance (DPGA) standard with open-source Apache 2.0 license, documented API, and transparent governance.
